Frontier intelligence you can hand real work

What agentic scores look like when the model never holds your keys.

Production · Arche 1.0 · The Belna team

Most people think AI is text in, text out. You ask a question, you get an answer, then you go do the work yourself. A brilliant model with no hands is still a chatbot.

Arche 1.0 combines open Kimi K3 weights with a Belna harness for approvals, sandboxed tools and secret handling. Moonshot AI reports benchmark results for the upstream Kimi K3 model across browsing, coding and agentic workflows; the figures below are not measurements of the full Arche harness.

How it works

The thread is simple. Intelligence is not enough. The harness is what turns a score into finished work you can trust.

Approvals by default

Apps, browsing and external writes pause for your yes — or an always-allow rule you can revoke under Vault → Approved.

Sealed vault

Secrets stay encrypted and masked in chat, traces and model context. Reveal is on your device, for your eyes.

Sandboxed doing

Code, browser and computer use run contained. Integrations are read-only via a per-request token — never stored.

Visible work

Every step is logged. Belna never pretends to browse, run code or read email unless the trace shows it.

Take
