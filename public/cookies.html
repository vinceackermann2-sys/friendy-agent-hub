Cookie Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

Belna uses browser storage to keep the app working. This includes local and session storage and IndexedDB, even where the browser does not call them “cookies.” The app does not currently set an advertising or analytics cookie in its own code.

  • Sign-in (lingon.session, local storage): holds the account session and refresh token so you remain signed in. It lasts until sign-out, expiry or clearing site data.
  • App state (lingon.v1, local storage): keeps chats, settings, local workspace state and references needed to resume the app. It remains until removed in the app or browser.
  • Provider preference (belna.lastProvider, local storage): remembers the last sign-in method for the sign-in screen. It remains until site data is cleared.
  • Pending invite (belna.pendingGift, session storage): carries an invite code through sign-in and is cleared after use or when the browser session ends.
  • Draft attachments (belna-composer-files, IndexedDB): temporarily keeps files selected for a draft on this device. Remove the draft or clear site data to remove them.

These items support requested functions and are not used for advertising. You can clear them in your browser settings, although that may sign you out or remove local drafts. Google sign-in, Stripe checkout, connected apps and externally loaded fonts may use their own technologies under their policies when you use those features. Clearing site data does not delete account records held on the server; see Privacy.
