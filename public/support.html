How can we help?

For help with your account, agent, billing or app connections, email support@belna.se. Include the email you use for Belna, your device and a short description of what happened. Please leave passwords, sign-in codes, private keys and sensitive health or contact information out of your message.

Sign in to your existing account

The web, iPhone, iPad and Mac apps use the same Belna account. Use your existing sign-in method to see your chats and tasks. If a sign-in code does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm the address before requesting another code.

Connect Apple apps

In the native app, open Apple apps using the Apple button or Connectors. Choose Calendar, Reminders, Contacts or supported read-only Health summaries. Connections are optional. Device permission and permission to share with AI are separate choices.

Keep the app open for an agent request to your device. Review requested changes on your device. You can manage access in Apple apps → Privacy and in your device’s privacy settings. If a change loses its connection before you see a result, check the Apple app before trying it again.

Mac reads the Calendar, Reminders and Contacts stored on that Mac. Check your Apple account’s sync settings if you expect information from another device. Health is offered only on a device where HealthKit is available. Notes, Mail and Messages do not have a general connection in this version.

Your data and account

Manage optional sharing and disconnect Apple apps in Apple apps → Privacy. To delete your Belna account, open Apple apps → Account → Delete my Belna account and confirm. If deletion cannot finish, contact support for help. Disconnecting a connection does not erase information you previously shared in a chat.

Read our Privacy Policy for information about processing, retention and your rights. For help with purchases or a subscription, use your account settings or contact support.

Report a problem

Tell us which screen you were using, what you expected and what you saw. A screenshot without private information can help. For a security or privacy concern, email support@belna.se.
