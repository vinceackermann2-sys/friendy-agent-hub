Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Controller: Belna, Stockholm, Sweden · support@belna.se

1. Data we handle

  • Account and sign-in: email address, user ID, authentication details, sign-in provider, Terms acknowledgement version and time, and account settings. Supabase processes authentication. Google also processes information if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Agent and workspace: prompts, replies, chat history, uploaded files, generated artifacts, memories, goals, tasks, automations, vault entries and settings you choose to provide or create.
  • Connected apps and agent actions: connection identifiers, permissions, content returned by connected services, action requests and activity records. Data shared with a connected service depends on the permissions and actions you authorize.
  • Billing and support: plan, token balance and usage, transaction and subscription records, payment identifiers, withdrawal requests, support messages and correspondence. Payment card information is entered on Stripe's checkout or portal.
  • Technical data: device and browser information, IP address, security and error logs, and browser storage described in our Cookie Policy.

2. Why we use it and legal basis

We use account, workspace and usage data to provide the features you request and manage your plan (performance of a contract). We use billing records to meet tax and accounting duties (legal obligation). We use limited technical and activity data to secure the service, prevent abuse and resolve faults (legitimate interests, balanced against your rights). If we ask for optional consent for a separate purpose, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Agreeing to the Terms or acknowledging this notice is not consent to unrelated marketing or optional tracking.

3. Recipients and international processing

Relevant data may be processed by Supabase (account and database), Microsoft Azure and AI model services (hosting and agent features), Stripe (payment), Resend (email), Composio and services you connect (integrations), and Google if you use Google sign-in or externally loaded fonts. Providers receive only data needed for the feature used, but prompts, files or connected-app content may be sent to an AI provider or integration when your request requires it. Some providers or their infrastructure may process data outside the EU/EEA. Ask support@belna.se

4. Retention and deletion

Account and workspace data is kept while needed to provide your account. You can delete individual memories, secrets and other items where controls are available; for a full account-data request, contact us. We may need to retain transaction records for statutory accounting periods and limited records to resolve disputes or protect the service. Browser storage remains on your device until you clear it, sign out where applicable, or your browser removes it. Deleting browser data does not by itself delete server records. Contact us for the retention period of a particular record.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email support@belna.seIntegritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY) in Sweden or your local data protection authority. Providing an email is necessary to create an account; other data depends on the features you choose. AI suggestions do not by themselves make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

6. Security and contact

We use access controls and security measures described on our Security page. No online service can guarantee absolute security. For privacy questions, requests or concerns, write to support@belna.se. See also Terms and Cookies.
