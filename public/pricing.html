How credits work

Every answer, search or build uses a small number of credits based on the work involved. Your balance is always visible under Billing. When credits run out you get an upgrade prompt — the agent never silently stops. Gift codes add credits when redeemed under Billing.

Subscriptions & gifts

Pro and Max are monthly subscriptions billed securely through Stripe — upgrade inside the app under Billing, manage or cancel anytime from the same page. The gift card is issued after your first paid month: a $50 card with Pro (100 credits), a $100 card with Max (200 credits). Share the code or redeem it yourself.
