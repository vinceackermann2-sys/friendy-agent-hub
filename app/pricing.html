Good to know

Your questions, answered

No need to be a token expert. Here are the basics.

What is a token?

Think of tokens as the fuel your agent uses to help you. Each time it reads your message or writes a reply, it uses a little of your allowance. A token is a small piece of information — for text, that might be a word or part of a word.

Your plan gives you a fresh allowance every month. Images and turning speech into text use tokens too. You don’t need to count them yourself: you can see what you have left in Settings → Usage.

How much can I do with my tokens?

It depends on what you ask your agent to do. For example, “Help me write a birthday message” usually uses fewer tokens than “Read this long report and pull out the key points.” Planning a trip with research and several follow-up questions uses more, because your agent has more to read and work through.

There’s no fixed number of messages per plan: both what your agent reads and what it writes count, including earlier parts of the conversation. Start free, try a few everyday tasks, and check Settings → Usage to see how far your allowance goes for you.

How long do my tokens last?

Your plan allowance refreshes monthly. How quickly you use it depends on your tasks.

A simple example: You ask, “Plan five easy vegetarian dinners for two and make a shopping list.” Suppose the whole task uses 10,000 tokens, including what your agent reads and writes. With Pro’s 100 million monthly tokens, that would use 0.01% of your allowance — enough for 10,000 tasks at that same usage if you used your allowance only for those tasks.

This is an illustration, not a measured token count for that prompt. Actual usage varies with the conversation, reply length, and steps needed. You can see your own usage in Settings → Usage.

Unused monthly tokens don’t roll over. Free resets on your signup cycle; Pro and Max follow your billing period. Check Settings → Billing for your next reset date. Tokens bought in a pack don’t expire and carry over until you use them.

What if I run out of tokens?

Any purchased tokens remain available after your monthly allowance is used. When your balance runs out, you can buy more in Settings → Usage or wait for your monthly reset to continue. Extra tokens don’t change the daily limits shown in your plan.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Pro and Max are billed monthly through Stripe, and you can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → Billing.
